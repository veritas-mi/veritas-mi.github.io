Privacy Policy

Data Collection

Veritas Market Insights collects stakeholder and entity contact information submitted voluntarily via our diagnostic intake forms. This information is utilized strictly to evaluate diagnostic scope and structure tailored engagement proposals.

Third-Party Processing

Intake form transmissions are securely routed via Web3Forms API endpoints. Commercial toolkit transactions are processed via Gumroad; we do not store or process payment card data on our servers.

Confidentiality

All client communications, competitor targets, and analytical parameters remain strictly confidential under institutional written engagement standards.
