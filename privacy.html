Veritas Market Insights collects only the information necessary to conduct scoped research engagements. This includes technical details, target operational parameters, and contact emails provided voluntarily via our Diagnostic Intake Engine.

All engagement records and diagnostic submissions are processed through encrypted channels and maintained under strict data firewalls. We implement industry-standard safeguards to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.
